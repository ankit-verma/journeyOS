Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025  •  JourneyOS Ltd  •  Effective: 1 June 2025

Summary: We collect only the information needed to provide our travel booking service. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

1. Who We Are

JourneyOS ("we", "us", "our") is operated by JourneyOS Ltd. Our data controller contact is: privacy@journeyos.com.

Jurisdictional note: This policy is written to satisfy UK GDPR / EU GDPR requirements. Users outside those jurisdictions should be aware that we apply these standards globally as a baseline, but local law may provide additional or different rights.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, hashed password, role.
  • Booking data: first name, last name, email, phone (optional), number of travellers, travel date, special requests, and computed total price.
  • Newsletter subscription: email address and subscription timestamp.
  • Usage data: standard server logs (IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamps) retained for up to 30 days.

We do not collect payment card data. No live payment gateway is currently connected; price figures are indicative quotations only.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account (legal basis: contract performance).
  • To process and manage trip bookings (legal basis: contract performance).
  • To send you booking confirmations and service-related communications (legal basis: contract performance).
  • To send marketing newsletters where you have explicitly subscribed (legal basis: consent — you may withdraw at any time).
  • To maintain security and detect abuse (legal basis: legitimate interests).

4. Cookies

JourneyOS uses the following cookies and browser storage:

  • jos_token (localStorage) — your authentication JSON Web Token. Session-scoped; expires after 7 days.
  • jos_user (localStorage) — a cached copy of your display name, email and role. Used to personalise the UI without an additional API call.

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and seek renewed consent where required.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements (e.g. hosting infrastructure).
  • Legal authorities where required by law or court order.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion to allow recovery.
  • Booking records are retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance purposes.
  • Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

7. Your Rights (UK / EU GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your account and associated personal data. See the Account Deletion section below.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — unsubscribe from marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@journeyos.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Account Deletion (Right to Erasure)

You may request deletion of your account and personal data by emailing privacy@journeyos.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will confirm deletion within 14 days.

Note: booking records may be retained in anonymised or pseudonymised form for statutory financial record-keeping (up to 7 years).

9. Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures: bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, parameterised SQL queries, and HTTPS in production. We cannot guarantee absolute security; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you within 72 hours in compliance with GDPR Article 33.

10. Children

Our service is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify registered users by email of material changes at least 14 days before they take effect.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: privacy@journeyos.com
